2. Create your GitHub account

GitHub is a website that hosts projects. Think of it as a filing cabinet for your writing that lives online and remembers every change you ever make. Basic GitHub accounts and GitHub Pages sites are free.

Step 1: Go to GitHub

  1. Open your web browser.
  2. Go to github.com.
  3. Click Sign up (usually in the top right corner).

Step 2: Choose your username

Your username becomes part of your website address. For example, if your username is dylanasdale, your free author site will live at dylanasdale.github.io.

Tips: pick something short and easy to remember. Use your author name or pen name if it is available. Avoid spaces and special characters — letters, numbers, and hyphens work best.

Type your chosen username and click Continue.

Step 3: Enter your email and create a password

  1. Enter the email address you want to use for your author work.
  2. Create a strong password and store it somewhere safe (a password manager or a notebook you keep at your desk).
  3. Click Continue.

Step 4: Verify your account

GitHub will send a verification code to your email or phone. Enter the code when prompted.

Step 5: Finish setting up

Follow the remaining prompts. You can skip optional questions about your interests — they are not required to publish a site.

What you just did

You created a GitHub account. You can now sign in at github.com anytime. Your account is where your author repository and your free website will live.
