5. Publish your free author site with GitHub Pages

GitHub Pages turns your repository into a free website. Anyone on the internet can visit it, and you pay nothing.

Step 1: Go to repository settings

  1. Open your repository on GitHub.
  2. Click the Settings tab (near the top right of the repository page).
  3. In the left sidebar, click Pages.

Step 2: Choose your source

Under Build and deployment, look for Source. Choose Deploy from a branch.

Under Branch, select main (or master, if that is your default branch). Leave the folder as / (root).

Click Save.

Step 3: Wait for your site to be published

GitHub will start building your site. This usually takes a minute or two.

  1. Refresh the Pages settings page.
  2. Look for a green box that says your site is published, with a link like https://dylanasdale.github.io/.
  3. Click the link to visit your new author site.

Step 4: Your site is live

You now have a free author website. The front page shows the contents of your README.md file. Edit README.md in your repository (see "Track your edits") and your site updates automatically.

What your site address means

If your GitHub username is dylanasdale and your repository is named dylanasdale, your site is at:

https://dylanasdale.github.io/

If your repository has a different name, your site is at:

https://dylanasdale.github.io/your-repository-name/

Troubleshooting

  • I do not see the Pages option: make sure you are the owner of the repository and that it is public.
  • My site shows a 404 error: GitHub may still be building. Wait a few minutes and refresh.
  • My changes do not appear: make sure you committed the changes, then wait a minute and refresh your site.
