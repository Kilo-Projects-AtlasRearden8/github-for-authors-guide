6. Publish excerpts from your book

An excerpt is a short sample from your book — a chapter, a scene, or a few pages you want readers to try before they buy. Publishing excerpts on your author site gives people a taste of your writing and links them to where they can get the full book.

Option A: Publish an excerpt as a page (simplest)

  1. In your repository, click Add file → Create new file.
  2. Name the file something clear, for example excerpt-chapter-1.md.
  3. Write your excerpt in the text box. You can use basic formatting:
  • # Chapter One makes a heading.
  • **bold** makes bold text.
  • *italic* makes italic text.
  1. At the bottom, write a commit message like "Added Chapter 1 excerpt".
  2. Click Commit changes.

Your excerpt is now published at:

https://dylanasdale.github.io/excerpt-chapter-1/

Option B: Publish several excerpts as blog-style posts

If you plan to share many excerpts, create a folder:

  1. Click Add file → Create new file.
  2. Name it _posts/excerpt-1.md (GitHub will create the _posts folder for you).
  3. Write your excerpt.
  4. Commit the file.

Link to each excerpt from your README.md or from a dedicated excerpts page.

Add an excerpts page to your site

  1. Edit your README.md file.
  2. Add a section like this:
## Free excerpts

Read samples from *The Massive AI Manual*:

- [Chapter 1 excerpt](excerpt-chapter-1/)
- [Chapter 3 excerpt](excerpt-chapter-3/)
  1. Commit the change.

Link to where readers can buy the book

At the bottom of each excerpt, add a sentence like:

Enjoyed this sample? Get the full book: The Massive AI Manual by Dylan Asdale.

Then link to your book's sales page (Amazon, KDP, your own site, etc.).
